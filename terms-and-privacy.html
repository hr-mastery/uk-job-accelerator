Section 3

Refund & 90-Day Guarantee Policy

Digilinx Advisory stands behind the UK Job Accelerator System with a full money-back guarantee, subject to the conditions below. We are confident in the system because it is built from the actual logic used to screen and place over 1,200 candidates across 20+ years of UK hiring.

Full Money-Back Guarantee — 90 Days

If you apply consistently using the system and do not receive a single interview within 90 days of purchase, we will refund your payment in full. No quibble.

The UK recruitment cycle typically runs 60–90 days to fill a vacancy, depending on role type, industry, and location. The 90-day window is set deliberately to align with this reality — it is a fair and generous timeframe, not a barrier.

Guarantee Conditions

  • Timeframe: Refund request must be submitted within 90 days of the original purchase date.
  • Application volume: The buyer must have made a minimum of 21 relevant, documented job applications per 7-day period throughout the 90-day window. This accounts for all working patterns including shift workers, weekend workers, and varied schedules — 7 calendar days is the unit, not working days.
  • Relevance standard: Applications must be reasonably matched to the buyer's professional background, experience level, and target role. Common sense applies — applications to roles entirely unrelated to the buyer's background and qualifications do not qualify toward the count. For example, a professional targeting logistics management roles would not qualify through applications to medical or legal positions.
  • Documentation: The buyer must submit an application log with their refund request. A simple spreadsheet or list showing company name, job title, date applied, and platform used is sufficient. No elaborate records are required.
  • Outcome condition: Zero interviews received within the 90-day period despite meeting all conditions above.

Refund Process

  • Submit your refund request via the Contact form on our site or by WhatsApp
  • Attach or share your application log
  • We review your request and respond within 3 business days
  • Approved refunds are processed within 7 days back to your original payment method via PayFast

Non-qualifying refund scenarios

  • Refund requests submitted after 90 days from purchase date
  • Refund requests without a documented application log
  • Applications not reasonably relevant to the buyer's target role and experience level
  • Fewer than 21 documented applications per 7-day period
